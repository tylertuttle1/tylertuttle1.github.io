I am a postdoc at the Czech Technical University in Prague. I'm part of the [G²OAT](https://ggoat.fit.cvut.cz/index.html) and [DiGeo](https://kam.mff.cuni.cz/~digeo/) groups.

I did my PhD at Carleton University, at the [Algorithms, Geometry, and Graphs Lab](https://cglab.ca/). My supervisors were [Prosenjit Bose](https://www.jitbose.ca), Darryl Hill, and [Michiel Smid](https://people.scs.carleton.ca/~michiel/).
